Nativelog

Time tracking for Jira Cloud whose data is the Jira worklog. Every hour is written as a native worklog under your own name, so it shows up in the Work log tab, in worklogAuthor = currentUser() and in native reports — and it stays there if you ever uninstall the app. No second copy of your hours, nothing to export, nothing to lose.
